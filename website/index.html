    What you can do with Buzz!

    • See who's around

      Your Buddy List is the home screen: Available, Busy, Away or Offline, live.

    • Send a BUZZ!

      One click shakes your friend's window with a sound. A 10 second wait keeps it friendly.

    • One window per friend

      Every chat opens in its own window. Put them anywhere on your screen.

    • Share what I'm playing

      Turn it on and the song you play shows under your name. Off until you say so.

    • Hangouts

      Ask "Play a game?" or "Study together?". No answer in 15 minutes and it goes away.

    • Friends only

      You chat with people you accepted. Put anyone on your Ignore List and they see you as offline.

    Help

    Is Buzz! free?
    Yes. Buzz! is free software under the MIT License. No subscription, no ads, nothing to unlock.
    Why a desktop app and not a website?
    A Buddy List lives in your tray, and each chat is a real window you can move anywhere. That needs a desktop app. Buzz! is built with Tauri and Rust.
    How does "Share What I'm Playing" work?
    When you turn it on, Buzz! asks your computer what is playing and puts the artist and title on your profile, next to your name. On Linux it reads any player (MPRIS). On Windows it uses the system media controls, and on a Mac it asks Spotify and Apple Music.
    Who can talk to me?
    People can find you by your display name or your exact email and send you a friend request. Only people you accept can chat with you or Buzz you, and you can ignore anyone.
    Are my messages encrypted?
    We don't claim that yet. We will only say it once it has been checked between two real computers on the live service.
