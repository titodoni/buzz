Designed Around People

Chat Built for Connection, Not Retention

Modern chat became an endless stream of notifications, feeds, and algorithmic retention loops. Buzz strips away the bloat and brings back pure human presence.

👀

Presence First

The Buddy List is your home screen. Instantly see who is Available, Busy, Away, or Offline. No guesswork, no ghosting, just real-time status.

💥

The Signature Nudge ("Buzz!")

When someone is away or daydreaming, one tap shakes their chat window and sounds an authentic chime. Equipped with a 10s cooldown to keep it friendly.

🪟

One Window Per Friend

Freedom from the single-pane prison. Open multiple chats, arrange them across your multi-monitor desktop, and multitask like a power user.

🎵

"Share What I'm Playing"

Bring back status personality. Buzz automatically detects what track you're playing in Spotify or your media player and broadcasts it on your buddy row.

☕

15-Minute Hangouts

No calendar invites, no awkward planning. Send quick invitations like "Coffee break?" or "Gaming now?". If unanswered, they gracefully expire in 15 minutes.

🔒

Mutual Privacy

Only confirmed mutual friends can ever see you or talk to you. No public directories, no stranger requests, no algorithms, and zero tracking.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Buzz free to use?

Yes, completely free. Buzz is open source software released under the MIT License. There are no subscriptions, ads, or paywalled features.

Why is Buzz a desktop app rather than a website?

Multi-window presence and native system tray integration require real desktop capabilities. With Tauri v2 and Rust, Buzz runs with tiny memory usage and true native windowing.

How does "Share What I'm Playing" work?

Buzz reads your local OS media playback (MPRIS on Linux, SMTC on Windows, and AppleScript on macOS) and shares only the title and artist with your confirmed friends.

Who can contact me on Buzz?

Only people you explicitly add by email and accept as friends. You cannot be found via public directories, search feeds, or recommendation engines.
