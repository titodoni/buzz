# Product steering — Buzz

## What Buzz is

A Yahoo Messenger revival, shipped as a **native desktop app**
(Windows, Linux, macOS) — not a web page. Chat built around people
being present, not around infinite scroll. The product argument: chat
got efficient and lost its soul, Buzz brings the soul back with modern
engineering.

## Rules

- Presence first. The friend list is the home screen. Who is online
  matters more than what was said.
- No feed, no algorithm, no infinite scroll, no recommendations.
  Never add them, even if asked.
- One tap, one action. Big click targets, plain words, no jargon.
- YM-era tone: warm, a little playful, never corporate. Status
  messages and now playing are the personality of the app.
- Nudge is the signature. It must feel alive: shake plus sound,
  instant, with a cooldown that prevents spam.
- Privacy is simple: only friends see each other. No public profiles,
  no stranger discovery beyond a direct friend request.
- Encryption claims must be true today. Until Encrypted Messages ship and
  pass the two-user check (docs/adr/0010), claim nothing. After that, the only
  wording is "Messages are encrypted on your device. Only you and your friend
  can read them." Never say Buzz hides who talks to whom, Presence, Status, or
  Now Playing, and never say a stranger or the server cannot see them.
